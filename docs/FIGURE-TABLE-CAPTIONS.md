The manuscript references the following canonical, regenerable files. The

## Main figures

### Figure 1 — `paper_results_overview.png`

**Scientific question:** Does source reversion evolve non-monotonically, and
does the source-patch effect change at the selected OLMo-2 transitions?

**Data:** `phase_a_summary.json`, `phase_a_7b_summary.json`,
`phase_b_deterministic_summary.json`, and `phase_b_7b_summary.json`.

**Caption:** OLMo-2 checkpoint-wise source reversion and selected source-position
residual-replacement effects. Left: deterministic 1B reversion trajectory over 24
checkpoints generated by the single reference renderer. Middle: separate-release
7B trajectory over eight checkpoints generated by the same renderer; the two
trajectory panels use a common reversion-rate scale. Templates A--C appear only
in the transition-level behavioral/causal audit on the right and do not define
the full curves.
Right: change in the early-depth source-donor patch effect at the audited 1B
late transition (templates A/B/C) and 7B transition (templates A/B). Curves and
bars summarize the canonical JSON artifacts; absolute patch effects and change
intervals are reported in Table 4. The figure supports a bounded
non-monotonic trajectory and selected local intervention effects, not a
universal token boundary or a complete circuit.
Shaded regions in the trajectory panels mark the selected late 1B and 7B
transitions used for the transition-level audits.

### Figure 2 — `semantic_pool_overview.png`

**Scientific question:** At frozen semantic peaks, do source gain and source-
donor patch effects persist across independent model families and semantic roles?

**Data:** `cross_family_semantic_pool.json`.

**Caption:** Conditional semantic source gain (left) and source-donor patch
effect (right) for Amber-7B and Pythia-6.9B on type/category, relation/slot,
and naturalistic axes, in single-token X-minus-Y logit-difference units. The
patch effect is the mean of item-level maximum layer shifts across layers. Bars
show equal-template family means, small markers show template estimates with
95% confidence intervals, and diamonds denote equal-family means. Family and
template checks, deterministic item-key halves, and donor controls are
summarized in the pooled artifact and Tables 5–6/S1. The figure uses frozen
peak checkpoints and
does not treat checkpoint rows as independent replicates; the pooled values
are conditional on the prespecified quality gate.

## Supplementary figures

### Figure S1 — `phase_a_curve.png`

Reversion (top), source-erased prior strength, and source contribution across
the 1B trajectory (bottom). The panels share the training-token axis; this is
descriptive co-movement, not a causal mediation plot.

### Figure S2 — `choice_decomposition.png`

Candidate-choice decomposition across the complete OLMo-2 trajectories. Lines
show the item-cluster means of (p_X), (p_Y), and (p_{other}); shaded bands
mark the selected late transitions. The figure distinguishes explicit-source
choices from parametric choices and distractors; it does not introduce a new
model evaluation.

### Figure S3 — `phase_b_deterministic_curve.png`

Layer-wise source-donor patch effect for conflict and neutral items at the 1B
2496B-to-2999B transition, in single-token X-minus-Y logit-difference units,
with the first-75%-depth summary boundary shown.

### Figure S4 — `phase_b_srcpatch_curve.png`

Layer-wise source-donor patch effect at the 1B global-maximum pair
(1909B-to-1993B) and the selected template-robust pair (2496B-to-2999B), with
the neutral diagnostic shown for context. This figure is contextual and does
not replace the corrected template-specific primary analysis.
