## Evidence inventory and provenance

- The authoritative 1B trajectory contains 3,840 rows and 121 unique item
  clusters; the independent 7B trajectory contains 1,280 rows and the same
  121-item key set. The supplementary fact-cluster key contains 28 tuples.
- `choice_decomposition_1b.json` and `choice_decomposition_7b.json` are
  derived, non-destructive summaries of the existing Phase-A rows. They report
  p_X, p_Y, and p_other after item-cluster aggregation and use
  10,000 item-cluster bootstrap draws.
- The primary causal transition uses corrected template-specific artifacts;
  the original B/C position bug is documented in `PHASE-B-RESULT.md`.
- The semantic pool is `cross_family_semantic_pool.json`. Its unit of
  inference is model family, with equal family and then equal template weight.
  Checkpoints are explicitly non-independent.
- The semantic checksum inventory contains 723 verified entries. The pinned
  container and job provenance are recorded in
  `SEMANTIC-PREFLIGHT.md` and the public-release checklist.
- Older entity-routing proposal files are not evidence for this manuscript and
  are intentionally excluded.
- The pilot generator supports an agree condition, but the canonical trajectory
  and semantic confirmation runs contain no agree-condition measurements; the
  manuscript states this boundary rather than treating the pilot schema as data.
